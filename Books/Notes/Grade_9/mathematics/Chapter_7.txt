 7.3.1 AA similarity  Theorem  
The AA similarity  theorem  for triangles  states  that if the two angles  of one triangle  
are respectively  congruent  to the two angles  of the other,  then the triangles  are 
similar.  In short,  equiangular  triangles  are similar.  Ideally, the name of this criterion 
should then be the AAA ( Angle -Angle -Angle)  similarity theorem, but we call it 
as AA similarity theorem because we need only two pairs of angles to be equal - the 
third pair will then automatically be equal by the angle s um property of  triangles.  The 
notation ~ is used  to denote similarity.
